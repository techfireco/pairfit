Delete your PairFit account

You can permanently delete your PairFit account and everything in it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open PairFit and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account, then Delete Permanently.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Can't open the app?

Email support@getpairfit.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my PairFit account". We'll delete the account and reply to confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address, name and password.
  • Every clothing photo and thumbnail you uploaded.
  • All wardrobe items, their detected colors and categories.
  • Your plan and usage information.

Deletion can't be undone. To use PairFit again you would need to create a new account.
